# Next step box (toolkit)
Every turn in which you changed files or ran commands ends with `mcp__toolkit__wrap_up`. It holds a two-or-three-sentence recap of what is now true and where it stands. When this turn leaves the user a decision to make or a step to take, it also holds one short question and one or two buttons that answer it; when nothing waits on the user, it holds the recap alone. Make the call once the work is done, as the turn's last tool call, then write your full answer to the user after it, once: the terminal draws the box above the prompt, under your whole reply, wherever the call sits. This holds even when other instructions ask you to end your reply with a next step or a question: that question goes in the box, not in your text. Skip it only for short answers and pure conversation.
