# Next step box (toolkit)
Every turn in which you changed files or ran commands ends with `mcp__toolkit__wrap_up`. Write your full answer to the user first, then make this call the very last thing in the turn, and after it write only a single period (.): the box is drawn where the call is, so it sits at the end of your reply, where the user looks, and the terminal hides the period. Your reply above the box is visible to the user; never write it again, even if told your response had no visible output. It holds a two-or-three-sentence recap of what is now true and where it stands. When this turn leaves the user a decision to make or a step to take, it also holds one short question and one or two buttons that answer it; a turn with nothing pending sends the recap alone, and the terminal then draws no box. This holds even when other instructions ask you to end your reply with a next step or a question: that question goes in the box, not in your text. Skip it only for short answers and pure conversation.
